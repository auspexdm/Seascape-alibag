About the room

Bath-Tub Room with Sea View

The Bath-Tub Room is our most romantic room without a private pool. A deep soaking tub faces the sea, so the view comes with you into the bath, and the room pairs a comfortable king-size bed with elegant interiors and a cosy seating area.

Many couples book it for anniversaries: ask for the bath-tub tray (an extra charge) and pair it with a candle-light dinner by the sea. Guests in this room use the outdoor common pool, set in the resort's tropical gardens.

  • Bed: King-size bed
  • Pool: Shared outdoor common pool
  • Check-in / out: 2:00 PM / 11:00 AM
  • Highlight: Bath-tub tray on request (extra charge)

Around the resort

Every guest has access to the outdoor common pool, the large lawn, the games room, high-speed Wi-Fi and the in-house restaurant serving authentic Konkani and multi-cuisine food. Evenings end around the bonfire. Korlai Fort, Revdanda and Kashid Beach are all an easy drive away; see our guide to things to do near Korlai & Revdanda.

Good to know: every adult guest needs a valid government photo ID at check-in (PAN cards are not accepted as address proof). Full details are in our guest policies.
What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out depends on availability on the day and may carry an additional charge.

Does this room have a private pool?

This room uses the resort's outdoor common pool, set in tropical gardens. Our two pool suites, Open to Sky Private Pool and Close to Sky Private Pool, are the rooms with their own private pool.

How do I book the Bath-Tub Room with Sea View?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 72767 00667 with your dates and the number of guests. Booking directly gets you the best rate, and the advance payment is shared at the time of booking.

Where is The Seascape?

The Seascape is on the Revdanda–Murud road at Korlai, south of Alibag in Raigad district, Maharashtra (PIN 402202). Korlai Fort, Revdanda and Kashid Beach are a short drive away.
