Travelling to Alibag with your pet

How to get there with a dog, beach tips, and how to plan a pet-friendly stay on the Korlai–Revdanda coast.

Journal · Travel tips · 5 min read

Alibag is one of the easiest beach trips from Mumbai to take with a dog. The right ferry gets you there in about an hour, and the quieter Korlai–Revdanda shore suits slow morning walks far better than the crowded beaches near town. Here is how to plan it.

1. Getting there with a pet

M2M Ro-Ro ferry: it sails from Bhaucha Dhakka (Ferry Wharf) in Mumbai to Mandwa and carries cars and pets, so this is the route to take with a dog.

Gateway of India catamarans (PNP, Maldar, Ajanta) are passenger-only, so they are not an option with a pet.

By road: about 110–120 km from Mumbai via Pen. Plan a water and walk break every hour or so.

2. Beach tips for dogs

  • Time it right: go early in the morning or after 5 PM. Midday sand gets hot on paws.
  • Carry the basics: fresh water, a towel and poop bags. Rinse off salt water before your dog goes indoors.
  • Keep a leash handy: fishing boats, local dogs and other visitors share the shore.
  • Skip the sea in the monsoon: stay well away from the water from June to September.

3. Choosing a pet-friendly stay

Always check the rules before you book. At The Seascape, pets are considered on request, so please contact us before booking. Where pets are permitted:

  • pets stay on a leash in common areas
  • pets are not allowed in the pools
  • any damage or extra cleaning is charged to the guest

The full rules are on our Guest Policies page.

4. Pet travel checklist

  • Vaccination record and tick and flea treatment up to date
  • Leash, harness and a name tag with your phone number
  • Food, bowls and your dog's own bed or blanket
  • Pet stay confirmed with the resort in writing
