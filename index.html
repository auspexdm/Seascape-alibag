The Resort

Where coastal charm meets refined indulgence

True indulgence is not only tasted, but deeply felt. Set against the serene backdrop of the Revdanda–Korlai shore, The Seascape pairs elegant ambience and attentive hospitality with the unhurried rhythm of the Konkan coast.

Every corner of the property — from the tropical gardens to the sea-facing terraces — is designed to slow your pace and refresh your senses. Stay a weekend or a week; you'll leave with sand still in your bag and the tide somewhere in the back of your head.

The Konkan Coast

Revdanda, Korlai & beyond

The Seascape sits on the Revdanda–Murud road at Korlai, on one of India's most underrated coastlines — where the gate quite literally opens onto the sea.

Korlai Fort, Revdanda's Portuguese ruins, Kashid Beach and Murud–Janjira are each an easy drive away. The Seascape is simply the best place to come back to.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability on the day and may carry an additional charge.

Where is The Seascape located?

The Seascape sits at Korlai on the Revdanda–Murud road, south of Alibag town: Revdanda–Murud Rd, Korlai, Maharashtra 402202. The gate opens onto the sea.

Which rooms have a private pool?

Our two pool suites, Open to Sky Private Pool and Close to Sky Private Pool, each have their own private plunge pool. All other rooms use the outdoor common pool wrapped in greenery.

Is there a restaurant at the resort?

Yes. Our in-house restaurant serves authentic Konkani cuisine alongside multi-cuisine favourites. A candle-light dinner by the sea can be arranged on request, and floating breakfast is available for pool suites on request at an extra charge.

How do I book a room?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 72767 00667. Booking directly gives you the best rates and personalised service. The advance payment is shared at the time of booking.

Are pets allowed?

Please contact us before booking if you plan to travel with a pet. Where permitted, pets must be leashed in common areas and are not allowed in the pools.

What ID do guests need to carry?

The primary guest must be 18 or older and present a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in: passport, Aadhaar, driving licence or voter ID. PAN cards are not accepted as address proof.
