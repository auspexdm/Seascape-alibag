Dining & Amenities

Authentic Konkani cuisine, candle-light dinners by the sea, and everything a slow coastal weekend needs.

The Kitchen

Konkani, by conviction

Our in-house restaurant celebrates the rich flavours of authentic Konkani cuisine — traditional recipes, fresh coastal ingredients, and expert technique in every dish, each one reflecting the true essence of the Konkan region.

The kitchen also serves multi-cuisine favourites for every palate at the table, from breakfast through dinner.

Floating breakfast available for pool suites, on request.
